A slower way to read the web

8 min read · Updated this week

Most of what we read online arrives in a hurry. Tabs pile up, links branch into more links, and the thing we meant to come back to quietly disappears under everything else.

Small rituals help. Keeping the few sites you return to within reach, leaving yourself a note on the page you were reading, and copying a link before you lose it are tiny habits that make the web feel calmer.

None of this needs a new app. It only needs a few helpers that stay close by and stay out of the way until you ask for them.
